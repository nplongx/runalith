# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims, provide actionable feedback, and keep discussion focused on evidence and the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate intimidation, doxxing and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's GitHub maintainer contact or moderation channels. Do not include sensitive personal information in public reports.
